A Little About Me

I'm Mansi, a creative and curious social media enthusiast who loves turning simple ideas into content that feels fresh and meaningful. After gaining hands-on experience as a Social Media Management Trainee Intern, I discovered how much I enjoy the mix of creativity, strategy and storytelling behind a strong social presence. I enjoy exploring trends, understanding audiences and finding new ways for brands to connect with people. I'm always learning, experimenting and looking for the next idea worth creating.
